# Creative Direction & Production Brief

## Video Bumper Profile --- Malam Penganugerahan Fatimah Al-Fihriyah Awards

**Visual style:** Luxury Islamic Cinematic Invitation\
**Core palette:** Emerald Green, Deep Forest Green, Metallic Gold,
Champagne Gold, Ivory\
**Suggested format:** 16:9 landscape, 1920 × 1080, 30 FPS\
**Suggested duration:** 45--60 seconds (adjustable to the number of
profile slides)

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1. Project Overview

Create a cinematic profile bumper for **MALAM PENGANUGRAHAN FATIMAH
AL-FIHRIYAH AWARDS**. The video opens with the event title, establishes
the prestigious atmosphere of the award ceremony, and then introduces
the profile slides supplied by the user.

The supplied slides will contain photographs and identity information on
plain backgrounds. Treat these slides as source assets, not as finished
visual designs. Integrate their content into a unified visual system
inspired by the reference invitation: elegant emerald-green atmosphere,
refined metallic-gold details, Islamic geometric ornamentation, premium
typography, and subtle cinematic lighting.

The result should feel like the opening sequence of an exclusive
international award ceremony: dignified, graceful, prestigious, and
celebratory.

## 2. Main Creative Direction

### Visual identity

-   **Primary style:** Luxury Islamic Cinematic.
-   **Supporting styles:** Royal gala presentation, premium
    institutional identity, contemporary Islamic geometric art.
-   **Mood:** Prestigious, elegant, warm, dignified, celebratory,
    timeless.
-   **Motion character:** Smooth, deliberate, fluid, restrained, and
    cinematic.

Avoid making the video look like a conventional slideshow. Each profile
should feel like a carefully staged introduction within one continuous
visual world.

## 3. Color Palette

  Color               Hex         Usage
  ------------------- ----------- ---------------------------------
  Emerald Green       `#087A4B`   Main background and atmosphere
  Forest Green        `#075B3C`   Secondary background and panels
  Deep Forest Green   `#03452F`   Dark areas, footer, transitions
  Metallic Gold       `#D8AD36`   Frames, lines, ornaments
  Champagne Gold      `#F2D77A`   Highlights and title accents
  Ivory Cream         `#FFF6E3`   Nameplates and light panels
  Warm White          `#F8F5EC`   Main supporting text

Use subtle gradients rather than flat fills. Keep the emerald center
slightly illuminated and the outer edges darker. Gold should resemble
refined champagne metal, not bright yellow or neon.

## 4. Opening Sequence --- Event Title

The video must begin with the exact event title:

**MALAM PENGANUGRAHAN FATIMAH AL-FIHRIYAH AWARDS**

### Suggested opening storyboard

**Scene 01 --- Establishing atmosphere (0--3 seconds)** - Begin on a
deep emerald-green field, fading up from near-black. - Reveal a soft
emerald radial glow in the center. - Fine Islamic geometric patterns
gradually emerge along the outer edges. - Add a few restrained floating
gold particles and tiny star-like glints. - Use subtle parallax to
create depth without distracting from the composition.

**Scene 02 --- Gold ornament reveal (3--5 seconds)** - Thin
metallic-gold lines draw in symmetrically. - A refined ornamental frame
or geometric motif forms around the central title area. - A gentle gold
light sweep travels across the ornament.

**Scene 03 --- Main event title (5--9 seconds)** - Reveal the event
title in a centered, carefully balanced composition. - Use elegant,
highly legible typography: a refined serif or premium sans-serif for the
main title, with selected words optionally accented in a flowing script
style. - Use champagne-gold or warm ivory lettering with restrained
metallic highlights. - Reveal the title through a smooth mask animation
or subtle light tracing. - Hold the completed title long enough to read
comfortably.

**Important:** Keep the wording exactly as supplied. Do not silently
correct, paraphrase, rearrange, or misspell the event title. If the
organizer later confirms a different official spelling, update it only
from the approved text.

## 5. Transition from Title to Profile Presentation

After the title has been held: - Let the gold frame expand, dissolve, or
transform into a fine gold light trail. - Use that trail as a visual
bridge into the first profile. - Keep the emerald-and-gold environment
continuous so the profile sequence feels like part of the same
production. - Avoid abrupt cuts, flashy wipes, or unrelated transition
styles.

## 6. Profile Slide Integration

The user will provide one or more slides containing photographs and
identity information on plain backgrounds.

### How to treat the supplied slides

-   Inspect each uploaded slide and identify the portrait, name,
    role/title, organization, credentials, and any other supplied
    information.
-   Preserve the original person's face, likeness, expression, clothing,
    and photographic details.
-   Preserve all names, titles, spelling, institutional identities, and
    factual information exactly as provided.
-   Do not invent missing biographical details, awards, positions,
    dates, or affiliations.
-   Do not redraw or fabricate logos, seals, badges, or institutional
    marks.
-   Use the plain background as an opportunity to separate the portrait
    and information from the source slide and recompose them into the
    emerald-and-gold visual system.
-   If clean background removal is not possible, retain the source
    background and integrate it using a tasteful panel or frame rather
    than damaging the photograph.
-   Do not crop important parts of a person or remove meaningful details
    from the supplied image.

### Recommended profile composition

A premium profile scene may use: - Portrait on the right or left, with a
balanced text area opposite. - A soft emerald gradient or deep-green
atmospheric background. - A thin gold frame, corner accents, or an
understated ornamental arch around the portrait. - A small gold divider
or geometric motif to separate name and supporting details. - Name as
the strongest text element. - Position, title, or affiliation in smaller
warm-white or champagne-gold text. - Optional subtle light behind the
portrait to separate the subject from the background.

Alternate left-right portrait placement only when it improves the rhythm
of a longer sequence. Keep typography, margins, frame language, and
color treatment consistent across all profiles.

### Suggested profile animation

1.  A soft gold light trail introduces the profile scene.
2.  The portrait reveals through a gentle mask or controlled fade.
3.  The name appears with a subtle upward movement and opacity
    transition.
4.  Supporting identity information follows with a short stagger.
5.  A restrained highlight travels along one frame edge.
6.  Hold the complete profile long enough for comfortable reading.
7.  Transition to the next profile using a consistent gold line,
    geometric motif, or soft dissolve.

Do not animate portraits with exaggerated facial movement, artificial
lip-sync, face reshaping, or generative alterations. The photograph
should remain authentic.

## 7. Motion Graphics Language

Use a restrained set of motion techniques throughout: - Smooth fade-in
and fade-out. - Slow cinematic push-in or pull-back. - Subtle 2.5D
parallax between background, ornaments, portrait, and text. - Fine-line
drawing and ornamental reveals. - Metallic gold light sweeps. - Gentle
floating particles. - Controlled glow and shimmer. - Mask-based portrait
and typography reveals. - Short staggered text entrances. - Soft
dissolves and elegant match transitions.

Movement should be slow enough to feel prestigious, but purposeful
enough to maintain audience attention. Avoid constant motion behind
important text.

## 8. Islamic Geometric Ornamentation

Use traditional Islamic geometric visual language with a modern, minimal
luxury treatment: - Repeating diamond and interlocking polygon
patterns. - Fine gold outlines with low visual intensity. - Symmetrical
ornamental geometry. - Small four-point or eight-point star accents. -
Delicate gold dividers and centered diamond motifs.

Place denser patterns near the edges and corners. Keep the center
relatively clean to protect text readability and portrait prominence.
Avoid random pseudo-Arabic lettering or invented religious calligraphy.

## 9. Lighting, Depth, and Materials

-   **Ambient illumination:** soft emerald light concentrated around the
    central composition.
-   **Gold highlights:** restrained highlights on borders, ornaments,
    and selected typography.
-   **Metallic reflection:** subtle moving reflections on gold elements.
-   **Bloom:** very light diffusion around highlights only.
-   **Depth:** soft shadows and layered parallax, without heavy 3D
    extrusion.
-   **Particles:** sparse, small, slow-moving gold dust.

The visual finish should be polished and refined, not glitter-heavy or
overly glossy.

## 10. Typography System

Use a consistent hierarchy: 1. **Event title:** elegant premium serif or
refined sans-serif; uppercase treatment may be used. 2. **Profile
name:** bold, high-clarity serif or sans-serif with strong prominence.
3. **Role and affiliation:** clean sans-serif, smaller size, warm white
or champagne gold. 4. **Secondary information:** restrained size and
weight, with generous spacing.

Ensure strong contrast and comfortable reading time. Avoid overly
decorative fonts for long names, job titles, or detailed credentials.

## 11. Editing and Information Integrity

This is a profile and award-event video, so accuracy is essential: - Use
only the text and facts visible in the supplied assets or separately
approved by the user. - Keep every name and institutional title exactly
as supplied. - Do not generate substitute logos or alter official
marks. - Do not fabricate credentials or biographical narratives. - Do
not change the identity or appearance of people in the photographs. - If
any text is unclear or unreadable, flag it for confirmation instead of
guessing. - Do not allow AI-generated textures, particles, or lighting
to obscure faces or information.

## 13. Audio Direction (Optional)

If music is added, use an elegant cinematic orchestral or ambient
instrumental bed: - Gentle orchestral swell during the opening. - Soft,
warm pads or strings under profile introductions. - Subtle impact or
shimmer accents for title and name reveals. - Smooth musical transitions
between profiles. - A dignified closing cadence.

Keep sound effects understated. Avoid aggressive trailer impacts, loud
whooshes, or overly dramatic percussion.

## 14. Technical Delivery

-   Aspect ratio: 16:9 landscape unless another format is requested.
-   Resolution: 1920 × 1080 minimum; 4K if source assets and workflow
    support it.
-   Frame rate: 30 FPS (or match the established project frame rate).
-   Suggested duration: 45--60 seconds, depending on the number of
    profiles.
-   Keep all text within safe margins.
-   Ensure names and supporting information remain readable on a
    standard display.
-   Use high-quality source portraits and logos.
-   Export a high-quality master file and, if needed, a compressed
    review version.

## 15. Negative Prompt / Avoid

Do not use: - Cartoon, anime, or illustrative portrait styles. -
Cyberpunk, neon, sci-fi, or futuristic HUD graphics. - Bright yellow
gold or cheap-looking glitter effects. - Excessive lens flares,
particles, or bloom. - Fast zooms, shaky camera, aggressive transitions,
or random rotations. - Overloaded geometric patterns behind text. - Fake
Arabic calligraphy or pseudo-religious symbols. - Distorted faces,
altered facial features, artificial expressions, or synthetic
lip-sync. - Invented biographies, titles, credentials, dates, or
institutional details. - Recreated or inaccurate institutional logos. -
Text substitutions, spelling changes, or unreadable typography.

## 16. Final Quality Target

The final video should look like a premium opening bumper for a
distinguished international award ceremony. It must combine the visual
richness of emerald green and metallic gold, the cultural refinement of
Islamic geometric ornamentation, elegant typography, authentic profile
photography, and seamless cinematic motion.

The event title must lead the experience. The supplied profile slides
must then be transformed into a coherent sequence of distinguished
introductions, while preserving the authenticity and accuracy of every
person and identity shown.
